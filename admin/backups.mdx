---
title: "Backups"
description: "Encrypted, passphrase-locked snapshots of everything."
---

**Backups** take an encrypted, passphrase-locked snapshot of everything: projects, clients, evidence, screenshots, users and their two-step login, and settings. Without the passphrase the file cannot be opened.

<Frame caption="Encrypted backups with restore">
  <img src="/images/admin/backups.webp" alt="Encrypted backups with restore" />
</Frame>

Keep a copy in your password manager. Moving to another machine is just install, sign in as admin, open Backups and restore. For per-project moves, use Export archive and Import archive on Projects.

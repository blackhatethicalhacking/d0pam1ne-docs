---
title: "Settings and company identity"
description: "Your firm's name, logo and house colours, used across reports."
---

**Settings** holds the company identity used across the app and on every report: the firm name, website, logo and house colours. Set it once and it flows onto report covers, accents and the interactive report.

<Frame caption="Company identity in Settings">
  <img src="/images/settings/company-identity.png" alt="Company identity in Settings" />
</Frame>

The local AI, backups, downloads and the credit line are configured here too. Re-run the onboarding setup any time to change the identity.

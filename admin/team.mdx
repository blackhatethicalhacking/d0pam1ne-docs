The **Team** page manages who can use D0pam1ne and what they can do. Add people, assign roles (Admin, Lead, Tester, Viewer), and see workload, activity and account security at a glance.

<Frame caption="The Team page: people, roles, workload and security">
  <img src="/images/admin/team.webp" alt="The Team page: people, roles, workload and security" />
</Frame>

Roles decide access: Admins manage everything, Leads run full projects, Testers run their assigned work, Viewers read only. Require two-step login for everyone, invite by link, and reassign unassigned issues.

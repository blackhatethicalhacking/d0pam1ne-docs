**AI memory** gives the local assistant lasting context: a per-project memory and a house style that applies to every project, so drafts sound like your team and respect your conventions.

## What it holds

- **Project memory** — context the assistant should always read for this engagement: scoping notes, client preferences, wording to avoid.
- **House style** — how your team writes: plain English, short paragraphs, business impact first.

## What the assistant can and cannot do

It drafts issue sections from evidence and templates, writes the executive summary, and explains impact and remediation, always under your review. It will not run or suggest attacks, mark anything verified or publish without your approval, and it only ever uses the local model.

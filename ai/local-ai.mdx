---
title: "Local AI"
description: "AI that runs on your machine, with no cloud and no telemetry."
---

D0pam1ne's AI is a **local model** run by [Ollama](https://ollama.com) on your machine. Project data, your questions, drafts and reports never leave it: there is no cloud AI, no account and no telemetry, and D0pam1ne refuses any AI address that is not on this machine. Downloading a model is the only time Ollama goes online.

The AI settings page recommends models for your available memory and shows what is installed. The AI powers issue triage, report drafting and the Ask AI assistant throughout the app.

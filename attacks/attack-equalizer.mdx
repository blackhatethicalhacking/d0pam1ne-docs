---
title: "The Attack Equalizer"
description: "Live traffic, signal and throughput while an attack runs."
---

During an automated attack the **Equalizer** shows the run as it happens: the signal from each tool, this machine's network traffic, throughput, and the raw tool output streaming in.

<Frame caption="The Attack Equalizer: live signal, traffic and throughput">
  <img src="/images/attacks/attack-equalizer.webp" alt="The Attack Equalizer: live signal, traffic and throughput" />
</Frame>

It is both a progress view and a sanity check: you can see download and upload rates, spot a tool that has gone quiet, and watch findings land. When Evasive Mode is on, the pacing state shows here too.

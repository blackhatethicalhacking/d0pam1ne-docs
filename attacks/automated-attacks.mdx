---
title: "Automated Attacks"
description: "The whole plan, run stage by stage, hands-off."
---

**Automated Attacks** does the thinking for you. It checks recon and, for each category that applies, builds the target lists and runs the tools one stage at a time, filing what they turn up into Needs Review, so you are not running everything at once.

<Frame caption="The Automated Attacks plan: stages, tools and targets">
  <img src="/images/attacks/automated-attacks-plan.png" alt="The Automated Attacks plan: stages, tools and targets" />
</Frame>

The plan shows how many stages apply, how many tools will run, and how many targets are in scope, with each category's reason (the recon signal that put it in the plan) spelled out.

<Frame caption="Automated Attacks running, streaming output">
  <img src="/images/attacks/automated-attacks-running.png" alt="Automated Attacks running, streaming output" />
</Frame>

While it runs you see the live pipeline, the current stage, issues filed and output lines, and you can pause or stop at any point.

The **Command Center** is the top-level dashboard across all clients and projects. It is where you see what is running right now, where the risk sits, and which engagements need attention.

<Frame caption="The Command Center across every client and project">
  <img src="/images/dashboard/command-center.webp" alt="The Command Center across every client and project" />
</Frame>

It surfaces open issues, reports generated, items in Needs Review, running scans, the AI triage confidence, risk by client and the issue workflow (Draft, Needs review, Verified, Ready, Reported, Fixed).

## Light and dark

The whole app ships in a dark theme and a light theme; switch from the top bar.

<Frame caption="The Command Center in the light theme">
  <img src="/images/dashboard/command-center-light.webp" alt="The Command Center in the light theme" />
</Frame>

Collapse the sidebar for a compact, icon-only rail when you want more room for the work.

---
title: "Client and scope"
description: "Set what is in scope, and keep a clean record of what is not."
---

Every project has a **client** and a **scope**. Recon and attacks only act inside scope, and anything you decide is out of scope is logged rather than lost.

<Frame caption="The Out of scope page keeps a clean record of what was excluded">
  <img src="/images/scope/out-of-scope.png" alt="The Out of scope page keeps a clean record of what was excluded" />
</Frame>

Names, IPs and URLs that recon and imports turn up outside the scope land on the **Out of scope** page. They were seen, never probed. Add one to scope and the next run includes it; log it as an observation for the client, or ignore it. Logged observations can be included in the report.

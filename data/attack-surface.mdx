The **Graph** tab visualises the attack surface as a network: domains, subdomains, hosts, services and live web, and how they connect. It is the fastest way to see the shape of an engagement and spot the dense, high-value clusters.

<Frame caption="The attack-surface graph of domains, hosts and services">
  <img src="/images/project-data/attack-surface-graph.png" alt="The attack-surface graph of domains, hosts and services" />
</Frame>

Filter the graph by node type (domains, subdomains, hosts, services, live web), click a node to focus it, and read the attack-surface summary alongside.

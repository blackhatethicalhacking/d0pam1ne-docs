**Project Data** is the single source of truth for what recon found. The **Hosts & services** tab lists every host with its open ports, services, versions and the issues attached to each.

<Frame caption="Hosts and services across the external and internal attack surface">
  <img src="/images/project-data/hosts-and-services.png" alt="Hosts and services across the external and internal attack surface" />
</Frame>

Everything is grouped by the **external** (reachable from the internet) and **internal** networks, with per-host actions to mark external or internal, purge, or jump to the services found. The tabs across the top move between Tactical Ops, Hosts & services, the attack-surface Graph, Screenshots, Technologies, Secrets, Credentials, OSINT, URLs and Sources.

---
title: "Out of scope"
description: "What was seen but never probed, kept for a clean record."
---

Names, IPs and URLs that turned up outside the project scope are recorded on the **Out of scope** page rather than acted on. They were seen, never probed.

<Frame caption="Out-of-scope observations, logged not probed">
  <img src="/images/scope/out-of-scope.webp" alt="Out-of-scope observations, logged not probed" />
</Frame>

For each one you can add it to scope (the next run then includes it), log it as an observation for the client, or ignore it. Logged observations can be included in the report builder, so the client sees what was noticed at the edge of the engagement.

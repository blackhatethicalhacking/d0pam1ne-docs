title: "Traffic log"
description: "Every URL that actually answered, deduplicated, with how it was found."
---

The **Traffic** page is the request-and-response log: every URL that actually answered across recon and the automated-attack tools, external and internal together, deduplicated, with the tool that found it, its status, size, type, response time and the IP it hit.

<Frame caption="The Traffic log of every URL that responded">
  <img src="/images/traffic/traffic-log.webp" alt="The Traffic log of every URL that responded" />
</Frame>

Harvested URLs that were never probed, or did not respond, are not requests: they sit on the URLs page under the Dead links tab. Export the log as **CSV** or **JSONL** for your own analysis, and include the first requests in a report as the Partial Traffic section.

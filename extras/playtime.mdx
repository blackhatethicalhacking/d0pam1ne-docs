**Playt1me** is an arcade for the wait: toys to play while a scan runs and you need a dopamine hit. Games like Game of Life, Snake, Pac-Man, Pong and Breakout, and visual effects like The Matrix, Plasma and Oscilloscope.

It is a bit of fun, nothing more, a nod to the name. Your scans keep running in the background while you play.

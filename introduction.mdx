D0pam1ne is a self-hosted penetration-testing workspace that runs the whole engagement in one place: reconnaissance, manual and automated attacks, OSINT, vulnerability intelligence and client-ready reporting. It pairs the open-source **D0pam1ne engine** (the recon and OSINT toolchain) with **D0pam1ne Pro**, the multi-user web application your team works in.

## What D0pam1ne does

- **Recon** maps the attack surface with a curated toolchain, with adjustable pacing and an **Evasive Mode** that paces requests and can route egress through Tor.
- **Manual and Automated Attacks** take recon's findings and run the right tools against each in-scope target, filing what they turn up.
- **OSINT** gathers public exposure: ASNs, people and emails, exposed assets, dorks, breach and infostealer data.
- **Reporting** turns verified findings into a branded PDF, an interactive HTML report, Markdown, JSON or Word.
- **Local AI** runs on your machine via Ollama: no cloud, no account, no telemetry.

## Editions

D0pam1ne Pro ships as the standard licensed build and a **Founder Edition** (Black Hat Ethical Hacking's in-house build), which unlocks extras such as Word report export. This documentation covers both; Founder-only features are called out where they appear.

<Note>D0pam1ne runs entirely on infrastructure you control. Keep the port firewalled to trusted addresses, and require login and 2FA on every network interface.</Note>

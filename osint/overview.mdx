**OSINT** maps what the target exposes on the wire. It is the stage below recon: emails, people, exposed host names, ASNs and email-security posture gathered from public sources, one stage at a time, filed into the OSINT area of Project Data.

## Stages

- **ASN and ranges** — the client's autonomous-system numbers and IP ranges.
- **People and emails** — names and email addresses tied to the domain.
- **Exposed assets** — assets exposed on the public internet.
- **Dorks and passive OSINT** — search-engine and passive discovery.
- **Email security** — SPF, DMARC and DKIM posture.
- **Breach and infostealer exposure** — credentials and infostealer logs tied to the client's domains.

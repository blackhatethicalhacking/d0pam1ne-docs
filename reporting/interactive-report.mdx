The **Interactive HTML** export is a single self-contained page the client can open in any browser: filter, search, sort and flip between dark and light, with its own save button. It carries the same house design as the PDF.

It opens on the risk posture, severity distribution and attack surface, with findings by weakness type and risk by asset, then the full issue write-ups below.

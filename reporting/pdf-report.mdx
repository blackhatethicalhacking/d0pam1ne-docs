**Download PDF** and **Open HTML** give the fixed-layout house document for sending or printing: a branded cover, confidentiality notice, the found-vulnerabilities list, and a full write-up per finding.

<Frame caption="The PDF report cover page">
  <img src="/images/reporting/report-pdf.webp" alt="The PDF report cover page" />
</Frame>

Each finding follows the BHEH anatomy: common information (probability, criticality, total risk, hostnames, CVSS), description, impact and risk, what an attacker can do, business impact, likelihood, technical description with root cause and how the weakness manifests, an evidence walkthrough, and the affected components and assets.

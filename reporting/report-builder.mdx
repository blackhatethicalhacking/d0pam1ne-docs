The **Report Builder** turns verified findings into a client deliverable. Pick a report type, fine-tune the sections, apply your branding and export.

## Report types

Presets decide which sections ship and what the download is named: **Standard**, **Executive Summary**, **Full Report**, **Compliance (ISO)**, **NIS2**, **All Issues**, **BHEH Complete** and **BHEH Overview**. Each tunes the included sections, which you can then toggle by hand (executive summary, methodology, scope, findings overview, full write-ups, attack surface, partial traffic, glossary, out-of-scope observations).

## Branding

Your saved company logo and house colours (accent and secondary) are applied to the report cover, section numbers and accents, in both the PDF and the HTML. Override them per client when you need to.

## Penetration testing type

Set **External**, **Internal** or **Both**; it is shown on the cover.

## Export

Export as **PDF**, **Interactive HTML**, **Open HTML**, **Markdown**, **JSON** or **Word** (Founder edition). Only verified issues reach the client; items not yet verified are held back unless you opt in.

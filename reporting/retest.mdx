---
title: "Retest reports"
description: "Show the client what they fixed, in a matching branded report."
---

After the client has had a chance to fix what you reported, a **retest** records what is now fixed, partially fixed or still open, and produces its own branded report, with the same cover, contents and credit line as the main one.

<Frame caption="A retest report: fixed, partially fixed and still open">
  <img src="/images/reporting/retest.webp" alt="A retest report: fixed, partially fixed and still open" />
</Frame>

The outcome summary counts each state at a glance, and each issue shows its new status so the client can see progress clearly.

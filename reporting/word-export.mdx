<Info>Word export is a **Founder edition** feature.</Info>

**Word** export produces an editable `.docx` of the report for the team to work on. It carries the same sections as the other formats.

<Frame caption="Export to Word from the Report Builder">
  <img src="/images/reporting/report-builder.png" alt="Export to Word from the Report Builder" />
</Frame>

<Note>Word export is still being brought up to the exact layout of the PDF, matching the chosen theme and every template option. For a client-facing deliverable, use the PDF or Interactive HTML, which are the finished house design.</Note>

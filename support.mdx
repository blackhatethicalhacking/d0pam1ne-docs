The D0pam1ne engine is built and maintained in the open by
[Black Hat Ethical Hacking](https://www.blackhatethicalhacking.com). It is free,
it stays free, and it keeps moving because people find it useful and say so. If it
saved you time on an engagement, here is how to give something back, from the free
things that matter most to the ways to fund the work directly.

## The one thing that matters most

If you do nothing else, do this: **star the engine on GitHub** and tell someone.
Visibility is what keeps an open-source security tool alive, maintained and funded.

<Card title="Star D0pam1ne on GitHub" icon="star" href="https://github.com/blackhatethicalhacking/d0pam1ne">
  github.com/blackhatethicalhacking/d0pam1ne. Give it a star, it takes two seconds and it genuinely helps.
</Card>

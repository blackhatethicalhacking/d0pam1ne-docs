The D0pam1ne engine is built and maintained in the open by [Black Hat Ethical Hacking](https://www.blackhatethicalhacking.com). It stays that way because people find it useful and say so.

## The one thing that matters most

If you do nothing else, do this: **star the engine on GitHub** and tell a colleague. Visibility is what keeps an open-source security tool alive and funded.

## Free ways to help

These cost nothing and make a real difference:

- **Star the repositories** and watch them for releases.
- **Report a bug or a false positive** with the request and response, a well-described issue usually becomes a fix.
- **Share what you found**, a write-up, a post, a note in your team's channel. Mention the tool if it helped.
- **Contribute**, a tool integration, a fix, a docs improvement. Pull requests are welcome.
- **Spread the word** in your red team, your community, your talks.
